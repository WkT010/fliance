1. What Are Cookies 什么是Cookie

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide information to the website owners. Cookies may be set by the website you visit ("first-party cookies") or by third parties whose services are integrated into the site ("third-party cookies").

Cookie是当您访问网站时存储在您设备（电脑、平板或手机）上的小型文本文件。它们被广泛用于使网站更高效地运行、增强用户体验以及向网站所有者提供信息。Cookie可由您访问的网站设置（"第一方Cookie"）或由网站集成了其服务的第三方设置（"第三方Cookie"）。

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control your cookie preferences.

本Cookie政策说明我们使用哪些Cookie、使用的原因以及您如何控制您的Cookie偏好。

3. Third-Party Cookies 第三方Cookie

We use services provided by the following third parties, which may set cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics — for usage analytics and traffic measurement. See opt-out instructions below.
  • Cloudflare — for security, performance optimization, and DDoS protection.
  • Intercom — for customer support chat functionality.

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing the use of your data. We recommend reviewing their policies. We do not control the cookies set by these third parties.

这些第三方有其自己的隐私政策来管理您的数据使用。我们建议您查看其政策。我们不控制这些第三方设置的Cookie。

4. Cookie Management Cookie管理

You have the right to control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser Settings / 浏览器设置: Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through their settings. Please refer to your browser's help documentation:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari
    • Microsoft Edge
  • Cookie Consent Banner / Cookie同意横幅: When you first visit the Platform, a cookie banner allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time.
  • Opt-Out Plugins / 退出的插件: Browser plugins like Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on allow you to opt out of analytics tracking.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality and performance of the Platform.

请注意，禁用必要Cookie可能会影响平台的功能和性能。

5. Google Analytics Opt-Out Google Analytics 退出

We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with the Platform. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit the Platform, what pages they visit, and what other websites they visited prior to visiting the Platform. We use this information to improve the Platform.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. This add-on prevents the Google Analytics JavaScript code on the Platform from sharing information with Google Analytics about your visit.

Alternatively, you can opt out by using the "Do Not Track" setting in your browser. When this setting is enabled, we will respect your preference and not track your browsing activity on the Platform.

或者，您可以使用浏览器中的"请勿跟踪"设置退出。启用此设置后，我们将尊重您的偏好，不在平台上跟踪您的浏览活动。
