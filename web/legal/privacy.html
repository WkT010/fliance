1. Information We Collect 我们收集的信息

We collect the following categories of information when you use the Platform:

  • Personal Information / 个人信息: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, nationality, government-issued ID (passport, driver's license), residential address, and proof of address.
  • Financial Information / 财务信息: Bank account details, cryptocurrency wallet addresses, transaction history, trading volume, and deposit/withdrawal records.
  • Usage Data / 使用数据: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, pages visited, time spent, clickstream data, and referral URLs.
  • Device Information / 设备信息: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings, and installed fonts.
  • Communication Data / 通讯数据: Records of correspondence when you contact our support team, including chat logs and email communications.

2. How We Use Information 信息使用方式

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Platform and its features
  • To verify your identity and conduct KYC/AML checks
  • To process transactions and settle trades
  • To communicate with you regarding your account, transactions, and Platform updates
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraudulent or illegal activities
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To personalize your experience and provide tailored content
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve platform security

3. Data Sharing 数据共享

We may share your information with the following categories of recipients:

  • Service Providers / 服务提供商: Third-party vendors who assist with KYC verification, payment processing, cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support.
  • Legal Compliance / 法律合规: Regulatory authorities, law enforcement agencies, and courts where required by applicable law or legal process.
  • Business Transfers / 业务转让: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the successor entity.
  • Affiliates / 关联公司: NEXA affiliated entities for business operations and service improvement.

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

我们不会将您的个人信息出售给第三方用于营销目的。

4. Data Security 数据安全

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption / 加密: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Sensitive data is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Access Controls / 访问控制: Strict role-based access controls limit internal access to personal data to authorized personnel only.
  • Regular Audits / 定期审计: We conduct periodic security audits, penetration testing, and vulnerability assessments.
  • Incident Response / 事件响应: We maintain a 24/7 security operations center and incident response plan.
🛡️ Our Commitment / 我们的承诺: NEXA employs bank-grade security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. / NEXA采用银行级安全措施保护您的数据。但互联网传输方法并非100%安全。

5. Data Retention 数据保留

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and for a minimum of 7 years after account closure, or as required by applicable anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing regulations. Usage data and analytics data may be retained in anonymized form indefinitely. You may request deletion of your data after the mandatory retention period, subject to legal hold requirements.

我们在您的账户活跃期间保留您的个人信息，并在账户关闭后至少保留7年，或根据适用的反洗钱和反恐融资法规的要求。使用数据和分析数据可能以匿名形式无限期保留。在强制保留期后，您可以请求删除您的数据，但须遵守法律保全要求。

6. User Rights 用户权利

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access / 访问权: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction / 更正权: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion / 删除权: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Portability / 数据可携权: Request transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Restriction / 限制处理权: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Objection / 反对权: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@nexa-exchange.com. We will respond within 30 days.

如需行使上述任何权利，请联系我们的数据保护官：privacy@nexa-exchange.com。我们将在30天内回复。

We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by relevant authorities, to protect your data. By using the Platform, you consent to such transfers. Where required, we rely on adequacy decisions or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

您的信息可能被传输到您居住国以外的司法管辖区并进行处理。我们确保采取适当的保护措施，包括经相关主管部门批准的标准合同条款，以保护您的数据。使用平台即表示您同意此类传输。在需要时，我们依据充分性认定或其他合法传输机制。
